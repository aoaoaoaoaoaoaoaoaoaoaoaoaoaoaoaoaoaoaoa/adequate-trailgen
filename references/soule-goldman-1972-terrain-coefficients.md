## Synopsis

The work measures the multiplicative metabolic cost of terrain relative to a hard surface. Its conventional coefficients are 1.0 for blacktop, 1.1 for dirt road, 1.2 for light brush, 1.5 for heavy brush, 1.8 for swampy bog, and 2.1 for loose sand. Later models use these factors to scale locomotion cost.

## Source Assessment

The full article was not inspected. Later field work warns that one coefficient per named terrain can vary with speed, load, slope, traction, and the actual roughness hidden by labels such as “dirt road.”
