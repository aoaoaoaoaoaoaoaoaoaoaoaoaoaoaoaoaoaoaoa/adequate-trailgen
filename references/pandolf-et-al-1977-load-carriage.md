## Synopsis

The Pandolf equation predicts metabolic power from body mass, carried load, walking speed, percent grade, and a multiplicative terrain coefficient. It became a standard operational model for load carriage and incorporates the Soule–Goldman terrain table.

## Source Assessment

The model was designed around standing and slow loaded walking. It is known to model downhill travel poorly and should not be treated as a universal hiking cost law.
