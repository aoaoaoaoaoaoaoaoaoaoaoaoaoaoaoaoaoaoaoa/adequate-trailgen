## Synopsis

The guidance uses 4 horizontal km/h, 300 vertical m/h ascending, and 500 vertical m/h descending. Horizontal and vertical times are computed separately; half the smaller is added to the larger. It identifies DIN 33466 and the Alpine Clubs' trail manual as its basis.

## Source Assessment

This is a walking-time signage convention, not an additive energy model. The half-smaller composition rule prevents representing the standard exactly as independent per-segment flat-distance penalties.
