## Synopsis

Petzoldt defined an energy mile as the energy required to walk one flat mile and recommended adding two energy miles per 1,000 feet of ascent. The study measured flat and 1,000-foot ascent conditions with and without a 44.5-pound pack. Its aggregate result was 1.6 equivalent miles per 1,000 feet, with sex-specific values from 1.32 to 2.02.
