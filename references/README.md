# References

| Work | Status | Pointer |
|------|--------|---------|
| [Naismith, 1892, Cruach Ardran, Stobinian, and Ben More](naismith-1892-cruach-ardran.md) | Metadata only; original journal note | Introduces the distance-plus-ascent rule now called Naismith's rule. |
| [Tobler, 1993, Three Presentations on Geographical Analysis and Modeling](tobler-1993-geographical-analysis.md) | Metadata only; technical report | Introduces an anisotropic slope-to-walking-speed function derived from Imhof's empirical data. |
| [Soule and Goldman, 1972, Terrain Coefficients for Energy Cost Prediction](soule-goldman-1972-terrain-coefficients.md) | Metadata only; journal article | Establishes the terrain multipliers later used in the Pandolf load-carriage model. |
| [Pandolf, Givoni, and Goldman, 1977, Predicting Energy Expenditure with Loads](pandolf-et-al-1977-load-carriage.md) | Metadata only; journal article | Predicts metabolic rate from mass, load, speed, grade, and terrain. |
| [Minetti et al., 2002, Energy Cost of Walking and Running at Extreme Slopes](minetti-et-al-2002-energy-cost-slopes.md) | Version of record retained | Measures energy cost per unit distance from −45% to +45% grade. |
| [Santee, Small, and Blanchard, 2003, Application of Energy Cost Algorithms](santee-et-al-2003-load-carriage-field-data.md) | Metadata only; open journal article | Field-validates load-carriage equations on graded gravel roads and records their limits. |
| [Birrell et al., 2007, Military Load Carriage and Ground-Reaction Forces](birrell-et-al-2007-load-carriage-forces.md) | Metadata and abstract only; restricted article | Finds approximately proportional ground-reaction-force increases with added military load while showing that load distribution also matters. |
| [París Roche, 2008, MIDE manual of procedures](paris-roche-2008-mide-manual-v1.1.md) | Official version 1.1 manual retained | Separately grades hiking-route environment, navigation, movement, and time-derived effort; movement is a maximum terrain class rather than cumulative load. |
| [Troy and Phipps, 2010, Validity of Petzoldt's Energy Mile Theory](troy-phipps-2010-energy-mile.md) | Metadata and abstract only; subscription article | Tests the hiking energy-mile ascent equivalence against measured caloric cost. |
| [Voloshina et al., 2013, Walking on Uneven Terrain](voloshina-et-al-2013-uneven-terrain.md) | Full-text inspection; no artifact retained | Finds substantial changes in joint work, muscle activation, gait variability, and metabolic cost on modest manufactured unevenness. |
| [Haddad et al., 2017, Session-RPE Method](haddad-et-al-2017-session-rpe-review.md) | CC BY 4.0 version of record retained | Reviews the duration-times-CR10 convention as a practical retrospective training-load measure and catalogs its subjective influences. |
| [Nuckols et al., 2020, Lower-Limb Joint Mechanics on Slopes](nuckols-et-al-2020-slope-joint-mechanics.md) | CC BY 4.0 version of record retained | Measures mass-normalized positive and negative ankle, knee, and hip power while walking and running across uphill and downhill grades. |
| [Hu et al., 2020, Eccentric Contractions in Sloped Walking](hu-et-al-2020-slope-eccentric-contractions.md) | CC BY 4.0 version of record retained | Models increasing lower-limb eccentric work downhill and at high uphill grades, with the largest downhill changes in knee extensors. |
| [Wood et al., 2023, Improved Prediction of Hiking Speeds](wood-et-al-2023-improved-hiking-speeds.md) | CC BY 4.0 version of record retained | Fits and compares walking-speed models against almost 88,000 km of GPS tracks. |
| [Houtmeyers et al., 2023, Differential RPE under Low and High Impacts](houtmeyers-et-al-2023-differential-rpe-impacts.md) | Metadata and abstract only; subscription article | Finds that leg-muscle RPE does not isolate imposed lower-extremity impact from other perceived exertion. |
| [Herrmann et al., 2024, Adult Compendium of Physical Activities](herrmann-et-al-2024-adult-compendium.md) | Metadata and official table only | Supplies standardized MET values for 1,114 activities, including hiking and graded walking. |
| [Austrian Alpine Club, walking-time guidance](austrian-alpine-club-walking-time.md) | Official online guidance | Documents the DIN 33466 distance/ascent/descent convention used for walking-time signs. |
| [UTMB World Series, kilometer-effort](utmb-kilometer-effort.md) | Official online rule | Defines trail-running race distance as horizontal kilometers plus one kilometer per 100 m ascent. |
| [Scheltinga et al., 2026, Agreement among Training-Load Metrics](scheltinga-et-al-2026-training-load-metrics.md) | CC BY 4.0 version of record retained | Finds weak-to-moderate agreement among subjective, physiological, and force-based running load; the latter requires per-step sensors and a disputed fatigue exponent. |
