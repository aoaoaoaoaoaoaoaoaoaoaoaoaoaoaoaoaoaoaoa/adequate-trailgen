## Synopsis

Naismith reports a ten-mile, 6,300-foot outing and proposes one hour per three map miles plus one hour per 2,000 feet of ascent for fit walkers on easy expeditions. The rule predicts time rather than metabolic energy and contains no descent or terrain term.

## Source Assessment

The rule is an enduring operational heuristic, not a controlled physiological result. Its original units imply about 0.79 km of flat travel per 100 m ascent; the common metric rendering of 5 km/h plus ten minutes per 100 m implies 0.83 km.
