## Synopsis

UTMB classifies trail-running race distance as `distance_km + ascent_m / 100`: every 100 m of ascent adds one kilometer-effort. Descent, surface, technicality, and load do not enter the measure.

## Source Assessment

Kilometer-effort is a legible and widely operationalized extensive convention. It classifies races rather than measuring metabolic energy and is calibrated to trail running, not hiking.
