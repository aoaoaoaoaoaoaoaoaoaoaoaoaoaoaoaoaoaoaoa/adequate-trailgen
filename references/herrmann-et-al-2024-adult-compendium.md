## Synopsis

The Compendium standardizes energy intensity for 1,114 activities as metabolic equivalents of task. Relevant entries include 3.8 MET for level firm-surface walking at 2.8–3.4 mph, 5.3 for normal hiking through fields and hillsides, 6.0 for cross-country hiking, 7.0 for backpacking, 7.0 for unloaded hill climbing at 6–10% grade, and 8.8 at 11–20% grade.

MET expresses metabolic power relative to rest. Total energy requires duration, so ratios between entries with different self-selected speeds do not directly yield equivalent distance.

## Source Assessment

The table is authoritative for activity classification and useful as a sanity check. Its broad activity labels are too coarse to serve as a segment-level route cost model.
